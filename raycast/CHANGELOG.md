# Hyperodds Changelog

## [Initial Version] - {PR_MERGE_DATE}

- Search Markets finds HIP-4 markets by ticker, team, event or pasted link, with a detail pane for prices, the fair line, the edge after fees and settlement.
- Top Edges lists the markets priced furthest below their fair line, after fees on a $100 buy.
- New Listings shows the newest HIP-4 markets from every deployer.
- Pinned Prices keeps up to ten pinned markets' prices in the macOS menu bar, refreshed about once a minute.
