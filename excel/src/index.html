Hyperodds for Excel

Live HIP-4 odds in your spreadsheet

Type =HYPERODDS.MARKET("btc") in A1 for a live table of markets, then =HYPERODDS.PRICE(A2) in the first free column. The cell follows the market on Hyperliquid, in cents. On every market Hyperodds prices, the fair line and the edge after fees update the same way.

Functions

=HYPERODDS.PRICE(outcome, [side], [quote])
The price of one side in cents, from 0 to 100. side is 0 or "Yes" (the default), 1 or "No", or the side's name. quote is "mid" (the default), "bid", "ask" or "last". The mid shows #N/A when one side of the book is empty or the bid and ask are more than 10 points apart. Once a market settles it shows 100 for the side that won and 0 for the other. The Status column of MARKET shows whether a game is in play or settlement is overdue.
=HYPERODDS.FAIR(outcome, [side])
The Hyperodds fair line in cents: its estimate of the chance that the side wins.
=HYPERODDS.EDGE(outcome, [side])
The edge in points (cents) on a $100 buy of the side: the fair line minus the price you'd pay at the ask, fees included. Leave out the side to get the better of the two.
=HYPERODDS.MARKET([search], [limit])
A live table of the markets that match the search, busiest first, 20 rows unless you set a limit of up to 100. Each row has the outcome id, market, status (Open, In play, Started 25m ago, Settlement was due 2h ago, Settled), deployer, side, bid, ask and fair line in cents, the edge side and its points, contracts traded in 24 hours, a game's start or a decision's time, and when the market settles. Times are in UTC and arrive as text, because a custom function can't format its cell as a date.

An outcome id is the number at the end of a market's link, usehyperodds.com/m/<id>. You can paste the link itself instead.

Install

  1. Download manifest.xml.
  2. Open a workbook in Excel on the web at office.com. A free Microsoft account works.
  3. Select Home, then Add-ins, then More Settings, and choose Upload My Add-in.
  4. Upload manifest.xml. A Hyperodds group appears on the Home tab.
  5. Type =HYPERODDS.MARKET("btc") in a cell.

Excel keeps an uploaded add-in in your browser's storage, so after clearing site data or switching browsers you upload it again.

Office lists the add-in as able to read and change your workbook, the permission Excel requires of every add-in with custom functions. The add-in writes each result to the cell that asked for it and leaves the rest of the workbook alone.
